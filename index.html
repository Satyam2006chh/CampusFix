The Flow

How CampusFix Works

A seamless loop from complaint to resolution — with full accountability at every step.

Step 01

Student Reports

Submit a structured complaint with photo, location, and category. Duplicate detection happens instantly.

Step 02

Auto Routed

Complaint is automatically sent to the correct department based on the selected category.

Step 03

Assigned & Fixed

Dept Head assigns task to staff. Student sees who is working on their issue in real-time.

Step 04

Student Confirms

After the department marks it resolved, the student confirms YES or NO. Only then is it truly closed.

About CampusFix

One Platform.
Every Campus Problem.

CampusFix is a centralized college infrastructure management system designed to eliminate the chaos of verbal complaints, forgotten reports, and unaccountable departments.

Built for three distinct roles — Students, Department Heads, and Super Admins — each with their own dashboards, responsibilities, and AI capabilities.

The Problem

Why Does CampusFix Exist?

Campus infrastructure management is broken. Here's exactly what we're fixing.

Verbal Complaints Get Forgotten

Students report problems verbally to wardens. With hundreds of students and issues, complaints get delayed or overlooked.

No Complaint Tracking

After reporting, students don't know: Was it registered? Who is handling it? When will it be fixed?

Don't Know Whom to Approach

Is it the warden? Faculty? Maintenance? Electrical? No one should figure out the college hierarchy just to report a broken cooler.

Problems Are Everywhere

Issues happen in classrooms, labs, washrooms, corridors, IT infrastructure, and security — with no single place to report.

No Centralized Data

Nobody can see how many problems exist, which departments have the most complaints, or which are overdue.

Duplicate Complaints

If 10 students find the same broken cooler, all 10 report it separately. CampusFix detects duplicates and lets students upvote instead.

Zero Accountability

If a complaint stays unresolved for weeks, nobody is held responsible. No deadlines, no SLAs, no escalation paths.

No Useful Data for Authorities

100 Wi-Fi complaints in Block B is not 100 individual issues — it's a campus-level infrastructure failure. Without data, nobody notices.

The CampusFix Solution

Student to Complaint to Correct Department to Dept Head to Staff to Resolution to Student Confirmation.

Instead of: Student tells someone, waits, asks again, waits again.
