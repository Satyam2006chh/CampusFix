About CampusFix

One Platform.
Every Campus Problem.

CampusFix is a centralized college infrastructure management system designed to eliminate the chaos of verbal complaints, forgotten reports, and unaccountable departments.

Built for three distinct roles — Students, Department Heads, and Super Admins — each with their own responsibilities, dashboards, and AI capabilities. Not just different interfaces, but genuinely different powers.

The Problem

Why Does CampusFix Exist?

Campus infrastructure management is broken. Here's exactly what we're fixing.

😤

Verbal Complaints Get Forgotten

Students report problems verbally to wardens. With hundreds of students and issues, complaints get delayed, forgotten, or simply overlooked.

❓

No Complaint Tracking

After reporting, students don't know: Was it registered? Who is handling it? Has it been assigned? When will it be fixed? Is it still pending?

🤷

Students Don't Know Whom to Approach

If a water cooler in Block B breaks — does the student go to the warden? Faculty? Maintenance? Electrical? Plumbing? No one should have to figure out the college hierarchy just to report a broken cooler.

🏫

Problems Are Everywhere

Issues aren't limited to hostels. They happen in classrooms, labs, washrooms, corridors, IT infrastructure, and security systems — with no single place to report them.

📋

No Centralized Data

Nobody can see: how many problems exist, which departments have the most complaints, which locations have recurring issues, or which complaints are overdue.

🔁

Duplicate Complaints

If 10 students find the same broken water cooler, all 10 separately report it. CampusFix detects duplicate complaints and lets students upvote existing ones instead.

👻

Zero Accountability

If a complaint stays unresolved for weeks, nobody is held responsible. There are no deadlines, no SLAs, no escalation paths, and no visibility into who dropped the ball.

📉

No Useful Data for Authorities

100 Wi-Fi complaints in Block B isn't just 100 individual issues — it's a campus-level infrastructure failure. Without data, nobody notices the pattern.

🚀

The CampusFix Solution

Student → Complaint → Correct Department → Dept Head → Staff → Resolution → Student Confirmation.

Instead of: Student → tells someone → waits → asks again → waits again 😭
