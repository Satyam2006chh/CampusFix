The Process

From complaint to closed — in 4 steps.

Every issue follows the same accountable loop. Nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Student Files Report

Choose category, location, urgency. Duplicate check happens instantly — upvote instead of filing again.

2

Auto-Routed to Dept

Complaint lands directly in the right department's queue. No manual forwarding, no lost emails.

3

Staff Assigned & Working

Dept Head assigns to an employee with a deadline. Employee marks it done from their portal.

4

Student Confirms Fix

Only the original student can truly close the issue. If unsatisfied, they reopen with a reason.

Features

Everything you need to run a campus.

Not just a complaint box. A full infrastructure management system.

Geo-Verified Attendance

Employees clock in and out only when physically on campus. GPS verifies presence at Chitkara University in real time. Off-campus departures during shift are auto-detected.

500m
Geofence radius around campus

Duplicate Detection

Same location + same category = duplicate. Students upvote the existing complaint instead, reducing noise and increasing signal.

Deadline Tracking

Dept Heads set deadlines when assigning. Overdue tasks are auto-flagged in red across all dashboards.

Real Analytics

Complaint volume by department, location, and time period. Spot patterns before they become crises.

Payslip Generation

Dept Heads generate payslips with basic salary, allowances, and deductions. Employees download their own printable invoices.

The Problem Space

Campus infrastructure is broken.

Here's exactly what CampusFix replaces.

01

Verbal complaints evaporate

Telling the warden in passing means the issue exists nowhere in writing. It gets forgotten in 48 hours.

02

No tracking after reporting

Was it registered? Who has it? When will it be fixed? The student has zero visibility after submitting.

03

No one knows who to contact

Warden? Maintenance? IT? Electrical? The hierarchy is unclear and varies by department.

04

10 students, 10 duplicate reports

One broken cooler generates 10 separate complaints. Without detection, all 10 get routed independently.

05

Zero accountability on deadlines

Complaints stay open indefinitely. No SLA, no escalation, no pressure. The staff feels no urgency.

→

CampusFix solves all of this

Structured reporting → auto routing → staff assignment with deadlines → GPS-verified work → student confirmation. Every step is tracked, every person is accountable.
